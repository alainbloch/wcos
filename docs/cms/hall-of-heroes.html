Best of the Best Competition Champions

Best of Best competition champions – This is a yearly competition run by the Head Kaiden to determine who is the best fighter in the Western Circle through a series of one on one duels. During these matches only the cleanest kills are accepted and each match is watched and judged by 3 Kaidens.

      The Way of the Contract

      All of those that have taken a contract swore an oath of completion, had a mentor, and had to train from 3 to 24 consecutive months to complete them. They are designed to help increase discipline and guide people to their next levels. They are not easy and you must have the support of your Kaiden before beginning one.

      • The Black Chain (Kaidens Joseph and Gad)
